## __Julian Philip S. Quijano__

FA5

Dataset source: World Happiness Report 2021

Given the 7 variables chosen for this study (Ladder score, GDP per capita, Social support, Healthy life expectancy, Freedom to make life choices, Generosity, and Perceptions of corruption), the Principal Component Analysis revealed a broad relation between well-being, socioeconomic development, and health. PC1, labeled "General Well Being through Socioeconomic Development and Health" was majorly dictated by Ladder score, GDP per capita, Social support, and Healthy life expectancy. PC2, unlabeled, emphasized Generosity, Freedom to make life choices, and Perceptions of corruption. Overall, PCA allowed the studying of the same variables and their summarization through fewer dimensions while retaining a sizable amount of the original variation (PC1 + PC2 explains 74.3%).
